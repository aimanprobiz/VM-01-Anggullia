About DailyBarakah SG

Empowering local Muslim businesses and enriching community connections in Singapore.

Our Mission

DailyBarakah SG is an independent community initiative designed to spotlight trusted local Muslim-owned businesses, exclusive Ummah special deals, and masjid activities across Singapore.

Masjid & Local Business Support

Through strategic partnerships with local masjids—starting with Masjid Angullia—we bring daily prophetic wisdom and community activities straight to your fingertips while encouraging worshippers to support neighborhood vendors.
